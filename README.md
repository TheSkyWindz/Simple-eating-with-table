# Simple Eating With Table

## Preamble

Are you tired of getting "Ate without table"? No? It's charming you say? Well, I think it's silly, so I did something about it!

While there are plenty of mods that REMOVE "Ate without table", I felt it was a pretty nuclear option and kind of just erased a vibe I liked, so I decided on a middle ground, with heavy inspiration from a user who wishes to remain anonymous

## What does it do?
- When eating without a table, no mood will be added- **Ate without table** basically no longer exists
- When eating WITH a table, pawns gain **Ate with table**, granting 1+ mood for 1 day (Same length as **Ate Without Table**)
- Will NOT apply to pawns with:
  - An ideology consisting of 'Roughliving: Welcome'
  - Pawns with the Ascetic trait
## Compatibility
**Safe to add/remove?**: Yes  
**Incompatible with**: Nothing but the obvious! (Mods affecting 'Ate without table', as this mod will prevent the mood debuff from existing)
