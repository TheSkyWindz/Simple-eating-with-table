# Simple Eating With Table

## Preamble

Are you tired of getting "Ate without table"? No? It's charming you say? Well, I think it's silly, so I did something about it!

While there are plenty of mods that REMOVE "Ate without table", I felt it was a pretty nuclear option, so I decided on a middle ground, with heavy inspiration from a user's
comment on another mod (If they wish to be credited, hit me up or comment on this mod, otherwise, I'll assume you wish to remain anonymous)

## What does it do?
- When eating without a table, no mood will be added- **Ate without table** basically no longer exists
- When eating WITH a table, pawns gain **Ate with table**, granting 1+ mood for 1 day (Same length as **Ate Without Table**)
